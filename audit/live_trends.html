How to read these figures

  • Counts describe the records BuildScope holds and when it observed them, not the full permit volume of any city.
  • A project count, a permit count, a trade-evidence count and a change count are different measurements of the same records and are never added together.
  • No record in this database predates the previous period, so no comparison is shown. A zero here would read as a decline and would be false.
  • No record carries a malformed permit date in this trade.

"New commercial projects" counts permits dated inside the window; it does not mean a bid is open, and it is not the city's total permit volume. Nothing here is projected or seasonally adjusted.
