What This Platform Is

A commercial construction lead is an active project worth investigating. Traditional lead directories assemble lists from scraped directories, stale phone books, and speculative estimates. BuildScope assembles intelligence directly from the public municipal and county permit records local building departments publish, attaches immutable provenance to every field, and states plainly where official records are silent.

The result is significantly higher signal-to-noise: fewer bloated records, each of which your estimating and business development teams can audit against official city filings before spending hours pursuing the project.

Who It Is For

Commercial specialty subcontractors — mechanical, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, structural — and the teams around them: chief estimators, preconstruction managers, and business development leads. Discovery is filtered by verifiable trade evidence, ensuring a {{ trade.short_label }} contractor only evaluates projects with documented mechanical scope rather than hundreds of unrelated residential roofing permits.

How Opportunities Are Discovered

Public records are ingested across regional building departments, normalized into unified data structures, and clustered by physical street address and parcel identifiers into underlying commercial projects. An official mechanical trade permit or explicit equipment scope in the permit description establishes relevance to this trade.

How Verification & Evidence Work

Every project is classified against documented criteria rather than algorithmic speculation. Fields an issuing city does not publish remain empty and render explicitly as Not verified. When two public agencies publish differing figures, both are recorded and flagged as a discrepancy rather than masked.

Monitoring, Alerts and Pursuit Pipeline

Watching an opportunity records your interest in it and raises an in-app alert when a later collection run detects a difference in the stored record. The record is re-read on each collection cycle, so a new trade permit filing, an issued status, or a plan revision is picked up when the issuing city republishes it. Teams can bookmark opportunities, keep private estimator notes, and track internal pursuit stages.

What this platform does not claim

A project appearing here is relevant to {{ trade.short_label | lower }} work because a public record shows mechanical activity. It is not a confirmed open bid. No source in this market publishes bid status, so the platform never states one. The pipeline stages are your own filing decisions, not a statement about a project's actual procurement status.

BuildScope does not claim that any project in this directory is currently accepting bids. Confirm all details directly with the issuing body, owner or general contractor.
