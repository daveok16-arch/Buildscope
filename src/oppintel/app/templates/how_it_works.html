Architecture

The 6-Stage Intelligence Pipeline

Each stage operates independently, preserving full provenance from official public records.

Stage 01

1. Collect

Public construction and permit records are ingested directly from verified municipal and county portals. Every response is archived verbatim before parsing so any change can be audited against official records.

Stage 02

2. Normalize

Source records are mapped onto standard schemas. Values a source does not publish stay empty. Nothing is inferred, manufactured, or estimated to artificially fill a gap.

Stage 03

3. Assemble

Individual trade permits and amendments at the same address are clustered into the underlying commercial project, preventing duplicate counting across structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing filings.

Stage 04

4. Attach Evidence

Every material fact is preserved alongside its publisher, record key, filing timestamp, and source text excerpt, creating an unbroken chain of custody.

Stage 05

5. Classify

Projects are scored against strict trade rules and assigned verified signals: HIGH, MEDIUM, or NEEDS_VERIFICATION. Every point awarded is documented with a clear, readable explanation.

Stage 06

6. Report & Monitor

Verified projects populate the opportunity feed, dossiers, and customer reports. Automation diffs records between runs to detect changes and notify watched projects.

Evidence Standards

Why Two Tiers of Evidence

We distinguish between explicit municipal trade filings and documentary scope descriptions.

Tier 1 — Mechanical Permit on File
The building department filed an official mechanical trade permit at the address. The City itself recorded mechanical scope, so no interpretation is needed. Strongest available signal.
Tier 2 — Mechanical Scope in Permit Text
The recorded work description explicitly names mechanical equipment (chillers, RTUs, ductwork, boilers, VAVs). Documentary evidence from the permit text rather than a separate permit.

Quality Gate: A project cannot be classified as HIGH on property class and declared dollar value alone. Mechanical trade scope must be substantiated by official documentation.

Integrity

What BuildScope Does NOT Do

  • We do not claim a project is an open bid. No municipal permit source in this market publishes open bidding status, and we never manufacture bid claims.
  • We do not fabricate stakeholders or valuations. If a source does not publish an owner, general contractor, or architect, we state Not verified.
  • We do not present finished work as active opportunities. Completed work is systematically identified and filtered out of active discovery feeds.
  • We do not confuse maintenance with commercial construction. Service calls, routine repairs, and like-for-like equipment swaps are excluded.
  • We do not create vanity scores. Every score corresponds to verifiable physical scope and documented evidence.

Important Procurement Notice

Permit evidence does not confirm that a project is currently accepting bids for any trade. Confirm procurement status directly with the project owner or general contractor, and verify all plans and requirements with the issuing jurisdiction prior to commercial commitment.
